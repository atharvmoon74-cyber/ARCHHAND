# HOUSEFORGE X — Practical Limitations

HOUSEFORGE is a **browser-based residential design and communication workspace**. Its site, architectural, structural, quantity, cost, and lighting tools are intended for concept development, spatial exploration, and educational review. The application is not a substitute for licensed architectural, structural, MEP, surveying, geotechnical, estimating, or building-permit services.

| Area | Current behavior | Required professional follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Site and plot tools | Editable dimensions, setbacks, road context, multi-resolution grid settings, roads, paths, parking, gates, fences, compound walls, landscape assets, terrain presets, and Shift-click conceptual elevation points are supported in the shared model. Terrain points non-destructively deform the conceptual browser terrain surface, but do not produce a surveyed grade, drainage design, contour plan, or earthworks calculation. | Confirm property boundaries, easements, setbacks, existing and proposed ground levels, drainage, utilities, and local zoning through a survey and authority requirements. |
| Units and precision | The shared model stores geometry in meters. Metric and imperial displays, including dimension-entry parsing and feet-and-inches input, convert values at the interface. Grid and nearby endpoint/corner/edge snapping improve conceptual placement. | Confirm all dimensions, datum references, tolerances, and survey coordinates with calibrated professional CAD/BIM and site-control processes. |
| House model | Walls, openings, levels, structure, room-programme overlays, finishes, interiors, lighting, services, landscape, and selected asset variants share one editable project model. Hosted door/window and linked-stair relationships reconcile basic placement after shared edits; floor-height edits propagate dependent level elevation before linked stairs regenerate. These intentionally simplified browser relationships are not a substitute for full BIM constraints, detailing, clash detection, egress, accessibility, fire, or code analysis. | Verify clearances, code compliance, constructability, room use, opening schedules, stair geometry, material specifications, and manufacturer details before construction. |
| Structural and project checks | The inspection panel identifies simplified model conditions such as missing foundations, unsupported-beam patterns, access openings, and envelope completeness. | Obtain calculations, load paths, reinforcement design, lateral design, and signed drawings from a qualified engineer. |
| Quantity and cost schedule | Areas and quantities are derived from simplified placed elements; rates are entered manually. | Independently measure all work, obtain local quotations, and include labor, procurement, waste, taxes, contingency, and market escalation. |
| Walkthrough and lighting | The walkthrough, daylight controls, sun-direction setting, and presentation mode visualize the same live project model. Daylight is artistic; the walkthrough uses a cutaway entry for browser visibility and does not simulate collision, gravity, stair physics, or door hardware. | Use physically validated daylight, energy, lighting, and visualization workflows for decision-critical studies. |
| Data and exports | Projects autosave in the current browser and can export editable JSON plus a text summary. | Maintain independent backups and produce professional drawings, schedules, specifications, and permit packages with appropriate specialist tools. |

> **Do not use HOUSEFORGE outputs as construction instructions.** All dimensions, assumptions, quantities, costs, checks, and visualizations require independent professional verification for the specific site and applicable regulations.
